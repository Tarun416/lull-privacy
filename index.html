Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Bitter Bite Studio built the Lull app as a free, ad-free application. This SERVICE is provided by Bitter Bite Studio at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

Local Data: Lull is designed as an offline-first application. Your stories, drafts, and notes are stored locally on your device using an internal database (Room). Lull does not require an account to create or read your stories.

Android Backup: Lull supports Android's system backup and device-to-device transfer. Your Lull stories and selected app settings may be included in Android's cloud backup or transferred directly to another device, depending on your device and backup settings. Lull does not operate its own cloud storage service for your stories.

Story Privacy: Lull does not send the content of your stories to our servers for storage or analysis.

Export and Import: Lull allows you to export your stories for your own records and import stories from a previously exported Lull file. Exported files are stored in a location chosen by you and are outside Lull's control. You are responsible for protecting any exported files you create.

Personal Data: We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your email or name to use the core features of the app.

2. Third-Party Services

Third-Party Services: Lull uses third-party services to provide analytics and crash reporting. These services may collect technical information about your device, app usage, and application crashes.

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

3. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
